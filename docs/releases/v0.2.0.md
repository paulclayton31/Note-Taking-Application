## Download and run

Under **Assets**, download the application for your Surface or Windows PC:

- **SurfaceNotebook-windows-x64.exe** — Intel/AMD devices.
- **SurfaceNotebook-windows-arm64.exe** — Snapdragon/ARM devices.

Double-click the `.exe`. No ZIP extraction or separate .NET installation is needed. Your notebooks stay in the same local library when you replace the executable with a newer version.

## New in v0.2

- **Automatic saving:** completed strokes, erasing, undo/redo, notebook names, and page names save locally in SQLite transactions on a background worker. The status distinguishes saving, saved, and retrying after a storage error.
- **Recovery:** committed notes survive an unexpected shutdown. Reopening restores the last selected page and shows a recovery notice after an interrupted session. Normal closing waits for pending edits to commit.
- **Notebooks and pages:** create and rename notebooks, add and rename pages, switch between them, and hide the sidebar for more writing space.
- **Touch navigation:** drag with one finger to move the page; pinch with two fingers to zoom. Zoom buttons, Fit width, mouse wheel scrolling, and Ctrl+wheel zoom are also available. Each page remembers its position and zoom.
- **Strict pen-only writing:** fingers and promoted mouse input cannot enter the ink collector. The touch-drawing and mouse-inking switches are absent. Touch also cannot erase notes. Palm navigation is suppressed while the pen is in range.
- **Handwriting preserved:** the same WPF real-time renderer, pressure-sensitive round pen, curve fitting, colors, and width controls are retained. Undo history now shares private stroke copies to reduce copying on long pages.
- **Existing page files:** Import page brings a v0.1 `.stempage` file into a new page without replacing your current work. Export page creates a portable editable copy.

## Saving and recovery

The library is stored at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SurfaceNotebook\library.db`. Keep the app open while it says **Saving locally…** or reports a storage error. **Saved locally** means queued edits have committed. A stroke still being drawn or an edit that has not committed cannot be guaranteed after a forced shutdown.

To copy the whole library as a backup, close the app normally and copy its `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SurfaceNotebook` folder. Individual pages can also be exported from the toolbar.

## Validation

Automated checks cover legacy page compatibility, SQLite transactions, ordered saving and retries, canceled-close recovery state, a killed subprocess with committed ink, multiple notebooks/pages, cache eviction and reopening, pressure and stroke identity, pen-only routing, touch pan/pinch, and preserving ink coordinates during navigation. The bundled x64 executable also runs a startup/layout/rendering/storage check before publication.

Physical pen latency, palm rejection, and touch behavior still need a session on actual Surface hardware. ARM64 is cross-built; its executable needs device testing. This release does not include PDF annotation, AI, cloud sync, or collaboration.

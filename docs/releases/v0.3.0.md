## What's new since v0.2

- **Home for your studies.** Notebook covers, nested folders, breadcrumbs, recent notebooks, stars, global name/folder-path search, and Continue writing. Create, rename, and move notebooks and folders from Home.
- **More room for writing.** A compact header holds notebook tabs and window controls. Focus mode, portrait layouts, page thumbnails, and a movable/dockable single-row palette adapt to the workspace.
- **Tool customization sidebar.** All seven tools remain visible. Tap a tool to switch; tap the active tool again for settings. Starting to write closes the inspector. Compact windows overlay settings without narrowing the writing viewport.
- **Saved pens and better colors.** Save named color/width/pressure combinations, use quick presets directly on the palette in spacious layouts, and choose from 18 swatches or a custom hex color. Pen and highlighter colors are independent. Existing ink keeps its attributes.
- **Lasso actions.** Tap a selection for Copy, Duplicate, Delete, and Paste. Mixed ink/text copies preserve content and pressure, receive independent identities, and can be pasted across pages. Editing actions support Undo/Redo. Finger dragging and pinching continue to navigate.
- **Undoable scribble erasing.** A tight repeated scribble can erase covered handwriting or individual typed words. One Undo restores the content and your scribble; a second Undo removes the mark. Turn detection off in pen settings whenever you want every scribble kept as ink.
- **Richer pages.** Highlighter, precision erasing, geometric ink shapes, typed text, physical paper sizes, graph/dot/engineering templates, page/notebook paper customization, and theme settings independent of paper colors.
- **Page workflows.** Touch zoom controls, remembered notebook pages, page duplication with independent objects, and transactional page reordering.

The native pressure-sensitive renderer, ordered autosave, committed-note recovery, and pen-only drawing remain in place. Fingers navigate without creating ink.

## Existing notes and upgrades

Your library remains in **%LOCALAPPDATA%\SurfaceNotebook**. v0.3.0 uses the same SQLite schema 3 and version 2 `.stempage` format as preview.3. The new writing preferences do not require another database migration.

Upgrading v0.2 creates **library-v02-backup.db** before migration. Upgrading preview.1 or preview.2 creates **library-preview2-backup.db**. These are consistent SQLite backups; notes, paper, views, tabs, and toolbar placement are retained. Versions that do not support schema 3 cannot open the upgraded database. See the [persistence and rollback guide](https://github.com/paulclayton31/Note-Taking-Application/blob/main/docs/v0.3-workspace.md#persistence).

Core note-taking continues to work offline. Wait for **All changes saved** before a forced shutdown. Recovery protects committed changes; an in-progress stroke or an edit still queued at power loss may not survive.

## Verification and current limits

Windows checks cover database migrations/backups, autosave retry and killed-process recovery, ink/text history, pressure/identity persistence, notebook organization, page workflows, tool-inspector geometry, color touch targets, mixed selections and clipboard paste, touch tap/drag arbitration, conservative scribble recognition, Undo/Redo, and restart persistence.

The release pipeline checks matching application/release versions, builds x64 and ARM64 standalone executables, verifies their embedded product versions, tests x64 bundled startup, renders the interface screenshots, and verifies uploaded asset sizes before publishing.

Physical pen latency, palm contact, gesture comfort, and sustained lecture use still need feedback from individual Surface devices. ARM64 is cross-built; automated native execution checks run on x64. Scribble recognition is conservative and can be disabled. PDF import/annotation/export, split view, STEM objects, AI, and cloud sync remain future work.

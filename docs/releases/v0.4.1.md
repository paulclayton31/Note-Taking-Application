# Surface Notebook v0.4.1

- Remove the floating Calculate actions popup entirely.
- In Calculate mode, lasso a problem with the pen or drag a rectangle with one finger/mouse. Lifting opens a preview in the existing right sidebar. Choose Solve explicitly to upload it.
- Two fingers cancel an in-progress rectangle and navigate. Switching tools, Esc, lost capture, page/view changes or pen proximity cancel a pending selection. Outside Calculate, touch navigation and hold-to-paste retain their usual behavior.
- New typed question is available in the sidebar. The latest-calculation close/reopen/replacement behavior remains available through Solution.
- Remember window position, size and maximized state across launches. Invalid or off-screen saved bounds fall back to a visible desktop position.
- Move Close from the bottom right to the top right of the calculation sidebar.
- Fix vertically clipped equations by measuring and translating the full glyph geometry bounds before layout. Fractions, subscripts, superscripts and text within equations retain vertical padding; wide equations scroll horizontally.

Windows checks cover direct gestures, cancellation, multi-touch navigation, automatic preview without upload, top-right Close, and rendered equation pixels at 100%, 150% and 200% DPI. Actual interface captures include compact and wide calculation sidebars. No live Gemini requests are made during these checks.

Resolves #18. Stable patch release with standalone x64 and ARM64 executables.
